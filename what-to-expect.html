The full process

How your defense moves forward

  • 01

    Screen, consult, and fee clarity

    Dallin screens whether your case is a fit, then walks through the charge and next steps in plain language on a free consult. Before you hire, you know what a flat fee covers — and what it doesn't.

  • 02

    Evidence review and your real options

    He examines the state's file for weaknesses and explains what each path means for your record and your future — in language you can repeat back.

  • 03

    Hearing prep, every time

    He checks in about a week before every hearing — even when nothing new has come back. You never walk into court blind.

  • 04

    He advocates for your timeline

    You shouldn't be forced into a rushed plea. When you're not ready to decide, Dallin will ask the court for the time you need, whenever that's possible.

  • 05

    Expectations before a plea — and a clean handoff

    Before any plea, he states plainly what you should expect next. When representation ends, you know what remains on you, including any probation or follow-up — no quiet fade-out.
