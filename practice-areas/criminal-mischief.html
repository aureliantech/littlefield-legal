What's at stake

The damage estimate drives the charge — and it isn't always accurate.

Like theft, criminal mischief is often classified by a dollar figure. That figure is sometimes inflated or based on repair estimates that don't hold up to scrutiny.

The valuation

Damage estimates can be challenged

Dallin reviews how the alleged damage was valued and whether that figure — and the charge level built on it — actually holds up.

Restitution

This can be ordered separately from the criminal case

Beyond any criminal penalty, you may face a separate restitution order to the property owner. He'll explain what's realistic so you're not caught off guard.
